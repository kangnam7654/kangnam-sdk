## [0.1.0] - 2026-04-21

### Added
- Initial import from `lunawave`.
- `CryptoService` — AES-256-GCM envelope encryption with random 12-byte nonce prepended to ciphertext.
- Convenience helpers `encrypt_string`/`decrypt_to_string` for UTF-8 plaintexts.
